About This Property

A modern mixed-use complex in the heart of Kamakis.

Kamakis Heights Apartments is a newly built 4-storey complex located along the busy Kamakis–Juja Road in Ruiru Sub-County, Kiambu County. The building sits approximately 800 metres from the Eastern Bypass junction, making it ideal for commuters heading to Nairobi CBD, Thika, or the Jomo Kenyatta International Airport corridor.

The property comprises 40 individual units spread across ground and three upper floors: 6 commercial shops on the ground floor, 10 bedsitters, 14 one-bedroom apartments, and 10 two-bedroom apartments. Each unit has independent KPLC metering, Nairobi Water connection, and fibre-ready cabling.

The compound is fully fenced with a controlled gate entry manned 24/7 by a security guard. CCTV covers all corridors, the parking area, and the main gate. There is a borehole backup for water and a shared backup generator for common-area lighting. Ground-floor shops face the main road and enjoy high foot traffic from the surrounding Kamakis estate.

The building was completed in June 2023 by Njogu & Sons Construction and is managed by the owner, Mr. Peter Kamau Njogu, with an on-site caretaker available daily from 7 AM to 9 PM.

Rental Policies

Before you move in, know the rules.

All tenants pay 1 month deposit + 1 month rent upon move-in. Deposits are refundable within 30 days of vacating, less any deductions for damages. A goodwill fee of KES 2,000 is charged for gate access card. Shops require 2 months deposit.

Rent is due by the 5th of every month via M-Pesa Paybill 4078832, Account: Your unit number (e.g., A1). Late payment attracts a penalty of KES 500 per day. Receipts are issued via SMS.

Tenants must give 1 full calendar month notice in writing (WhatsApp or letter to caretaker). Failure to give notice forfeits the deposit. The unit must be returned in its original condition — freshly painted walls and cleaned.

No pets allowed in the building. Subletting is strictly prohibited — tenants must be the occupants named on the lease. Any subletting discovered leads to immediate eviction.

Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 6 AM. No loud music, no parties without prior notice to the caretaker. Smoking is prohibited in common areas. Laundry must only be hung in designated drying areas.

Water is billed at a flat KES 800 /month for residential and KES 1,200 /month for shops. Garbage fee is KES 300 /month. Electricity is prepaid via KPLC token — buy at any agent. Fibre internet is arranged independently with providers.
