Green Valley Plaza is a mixed-use block on the Thika Road corridor, less than a 10-minute walk from JKUAT main campus and the Juja City Mall. The ground floor is zoned for retail while floors 1 through 4 contain furnished bedsitters and one-bedroom apartments — ideal for students, young professionals and short-term stays.

The compound is fenced with a perimeter wall and a 24-hour manned gate; it has two water towers (20,000 L each) with an automated borehole pump, and a 60 kVA backup generator for common areas and shop floors. Parking for tenants and customers is covered and open-air with a total of 62 bays.

All rental agreements are with the property owner; tenants pay KPLC, KIWASCO and a small service charge per unit.

A note from the listing agent

"Student-friendly, well-built and genuinely well-run. The vacancy rate at Green Valley has averaged 18% since 2022 — very low for Juja. The owner likes to keep rents below the street average to maintain long tenancies."
— Sarah Mwangi, Agent
