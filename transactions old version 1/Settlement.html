Holiday Policy

How settlement dates and operational windows shift when local, corridor, or central bank holidays apply.
  • Domestic railsMove to next local business day when central bank is closed.
  • Cross-border corridorsBoth source and destination market calendars are evaluated before estimating finality.
  • SEPA / TARGETNon-TARGET business days defer same-day cycles automatically.
  • Real-time railsMessaging may continue, but funding, oversight, and reporting can defer to next business day.
  • EOD closeHoliday carry-forwards are reflected in opening position preview and next-day funding plans.
