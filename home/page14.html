Section 14.2 · Origin Story

It started with a $2,400 wire that took 9 days.

In 2021, our founders were running an e-commerce platform with sellers in Lagos and suppliers in Shenzhen. A single supplier payment passed through four banks, lost 7% to FX spreads, and took over a week to arrive.

They realized the problem wasn't theirs — it was the infrastructure. So they built what didn't exist: a single API connecting African rails to the world.

Section 14.3 · Mission & Vision

Financial inclusion through infrastructure, not charity.

We don't believe in handouts. We believe Africa's builders deserve the same financial primitives that powered Silicon Valley — built natively for Africa's complexity.

Mission

Power the next 10,000 African fintechs, neobanks, and enterprises with the same primitives that built modern finance — without compromising on local depth.

Vision

A world where launching a financial product in Africa is no harder than launching one in San Francisco — and where capital flows as freely between Lagos and London as between New York and London.

North star

Every dollar that should reach a recipient on the continent does — instantly, cheaply, and with full traceability — through infrastructure we own from the ground up.

Section 14.6 · Company Values

Six principles that shape every decision.

01
Money is sacred

Every transaction is someone's salary, rent, supplier payment, or aid. We treat funds with the gravity they deserve.

02
Ship like the day depends on it

We measure velocity in transactions enabled, not features merged. Slow infrastructure costs builders months.

03
Africa is not a country

54 markets, 2,000 languages, 40 regulators. We honor the complexity instead of flattening it into one playbook.

04
Default to transparency

Fees, FX spreads, settlement times, incidents. If we can't explain it to a customer, we shouldn't be doing it.

05
Customers, not users

We build for the engineer at 2am debugging a payout, the CFO closing books, and the founder racing to launch.

06
Own the outcome

When a rail breaks, when a payment fails, when a regulator changes the rules — it's our problem to solve, not deflect.
