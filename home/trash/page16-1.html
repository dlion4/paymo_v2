Section 16.7 · Frequently asked

Fast answers. Before you even ask.

What's the average response time for sales inquiries?
During business hours in your region — under 2 hours for sales, under 15 minutes for live chat. Enterprise priority customers get a dedicated Slack/Discord channel with a 5-minute SLA.
Can I request an NDA before sharing sensitive details?
Absolutely. Just check the "I'd like an NDA discussion" box on the form, or mention it in your message. We'll send our mutual NDA template — typically signed and returned within the same business day. No sensitive PII ever leaves our encrypted form submission path.
Do you run pilot programs before enterprise commitment?
Yes. Most enterprise customers run a 2–6 week pilot in sandbox, then move to production. We'll configure a dedicated pilot environment in your primary market with free transaction volume up to a negotiated threshold. See the "Book a live demo" section above to start one.
How does pricing work for enterprise?
Usage-based pricing with volume commitments. Enterprise agreements include committed-use discounts, dedicated infrastructure, SLA-backed uptime with financial backing, and custom pricing for card programs and white-label deployments. Transparency is baked into every contract.
Can we bring Paymo engineers on-site for our launch?
Yes. Enterprise customers get a dedicated Solutions Engineer for pre-launch integration, and a Solutions Architect for go-live week. For high-visibility launches, we can fly engineers on-site to your primary market for the first 72 hours. Ask about enterprise "war-room" support during your demo.
What's the migration path if we're on another PSP?
We offer assisted migrations for teams switching from Flutterwave, Paystack, Maplerad, and similar providers. Our team provides a custom migration playbook, dual-run mode support (parallel processing during transition), and free transaction credits during cutover. Migrations typically take 3–6 weeks end-to-end.
Do investors get dedicated briefings?
Yes — investors evaluating Paymo or considering deployment at portfolio companies can book an investor briefing with our Strategy & Partnerships team. These 45-minute sessions include a CFO-level overview, market positioning deck, and investor-focused financials (under NDA).
What do I need to prepare before my first sales call?
A rough sense of what you're building, your primary market(s), and volume estimates is plenty. You don't need a detailed spec. Your Paymo lead will come prepared with specific rails, pricing, licensing paths, and integration timelines tailored to the persona you selected above.
