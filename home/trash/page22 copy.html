Section 22.1 · Scope & Commitment

We use tracking technologies only where they are necessary, useful, or consented.

Paymo uses strictly necessary cookies to secure accounts, keep sessions active, prevent fraud, remember consent choices, and operate developer tools. Functional, analytics, and marketing cookies are optional and can be changed at any time from the Consent Center.

Opt-in where required

Consent choices are not account security settings.
Disabling optional cookies does not disable MFA, transaction alerts, fraud monitoring, audit logging, or regulatory recordkeeping.

Section 22.7 · Browser & Device Controls

You can control cookies from Paymo and your browser.

Browser Settings

Block, clear, or isolate cookies through Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge or mobile browser settings.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends GPC, Paymo treats it as an opt-out from sale/share and marketing cookies.

Mobile Controls

Reset advertising IDs or limit tracking through iOS and Android privacy settings.

Preference Center

Use Paymo controls to accept, reject, or customize optional categories at any time.

Section 22.8 · Retention

Retention is limited by category.

Session cookies expire when your browser closes. Persistent cookies last only as long as needed for their purpose, consent, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Section 22.9 · Policy Updates

Material changes get advance notice.

We provide 30 days' notice for material changes to cookie categories, purposes, third-party partners, or consent processing logic. Previous versions are retained for audit and legal review.
