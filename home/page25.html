Section 25.1 · Scope & Purpose

One platform. Clear rules for everyone.

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to all users of Paymo Financial Technologies Ltd's platform, APIs, dashboard, mobile applications, and services. By using our Services, you agree to comply with this AUP in addition to our Terms of Use and other policies.

Why this matters

As regulated financial infrastructure operating across 25+ markets, misuse of our platform can harm real people, breach laws, and endanger our licenses. This AUP keeps Paymo safe for legitimate businesses.

Section 25.2 · Permitted Uses

What you can do.

Legitimate financial activities are welcome on Paymo. These include, but are not limited to:

Payments & Collections

Accept and disburse funds via mobile money, cards, bank transfer, and QR.

Cross-Border Transfers

Legitimate remittances and B2B settlements across supported corridors.

Embedded Banking

Build licensed neobanks, wallets, and white-label financial products.

FX & Treasury

Manage multi-currency balances and optimize conversion legitimately.

Payroll & Disbursements

Pay employees, contractors, suppliers, and beneficiaries.

API Integration

Build apps within documented rate limits and authorized scopes.

Section 25.3 · Prohibited Uses

What you cannot do.

The following are strictly prohibited and result in immediate enforcement action.

Money Laundering

Concealing illicit funds, layering, structuring, or smurfing.

Terrorist Financing

Funding terrorism or sanctioned individuals/entities.

Fraud & Scams

Phishing, Ponzi/pyramid schemes, advance-fee fraud, identity theft.

Illegal Goods

Drugs, weapons, counterfeit items, stolen goods, wildlife trafficking.

Human Exploitation

Human trafficking, forced labor, child exploitation material.

Sanctions Evasion

Circumventing OFAC, UN, EU, or local sanctions regimes.

Platform Abuse

Scraping, reverse engineering, DDoS, unauthorized bots, exploits.

Unlicensed Gambling

Operating gambling without legal authorization in the jurisdiction.

Unauthorized Reselling

Reselling Paymo services without a partnership agreement.

Zero tolerance

Engaging in any prohibited activity leads to immediate account suspension, fund holds (up to 180 days per regulation), law enforcement referral, and permanent platform bans.

Section 25.4 · Restricted Industries

Allowed with conditions.

These sectors require prior written approval, enhanced due diligence, and may face additional restrictions or higher reserves.

Industry Status Requirements
Licensed Gambling / Betting Conditional Valid local license + EDD + higher reserve
Cryptocurrency / VASPs Conditional VASP registration + Travel Rule compliance
Lending / Credit Conditional Lending license per jurisdiction
Forex / CFD Trading Conditional Regulatory authorization + risk disclosures
Adult Content (legal) Restricted Age verification + jurisdictional legality
Pharmaceuticals Conditional Licensed pharmacy + prescription controls
Money Service Businesses Conditional MSB license + AML program review
Charities / NGOs Allowed* Registration proof + beneficiary transparency
Approval required before onboarding

Operating in a restricted industry without prior approval is treated as a policy violation. Contact our compliance team before launch.

Section 25.7 · Enforcement

How violations are handled.

1
Detection & Review

Automated monitoring and manual review flag potential violations 24/7.

2
Warning or Inquiry

For minor/ambiguous issues we may request clarification or issue a warning.

3
Suspension & Holds

Serious or repeated violations trigger account suspension and fund holds.

4
Termination & Referral

Severe cases lead to permanent bans and referral to regulators/law enforcement.

Right to appeal

You may appeal an enforcement decision within 14 days via our Dispute Resolution process. Appeals are reviewed independently of the original decision.
