 ## 2. COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE MATRIX
Niobi (niobi.co)
Model: Payment Infrastructure + Business Accounts
Strengths: - 16+ African markets via single API - Direct mobile money integrations (M-Pesa, MoMo, Airtel, Orange, Wave, etc.) - Multi-currency virtual accounts (20+ currencies) - Instant FX with 90% cost reduction claim - Finance operations suite (AP/AR, payroll, reconciliation) - Xero/QuickBooks sync
Gaps Paymo Exploits: - No true embedded banking / white-label capability - Limited global payout rails beyond Africa - No developer-facing BAAS tooling (no card issuing, no ledger management) - No compliance-as-a-service layer
Maplerad (maplerad.com)
Model: Financial Infrastructure / BAAS
Strengths: - Global payouts in 10+ currencies - Identity verification (KYC) APIs - Virtual account generation - USD payment methods / card issuing - Lending, digital bank, savings modules - Strong “build your fintech” positioning
Gaps Paymo Exploits: - Shallow African mobile money coverage vs. Niobi - No pan-African treasury/cash management focus - Limited cross-border FX intelligence - No Africa-specific compliance automation (AML/CFT for African regulators)
Other Benchmarks
•	Flutterwave: Strong collect/pay, but not true BAAS; limited embedded banking
•	Paystack (Stripe): Excellent developer UX, but Nigeria/Ghana heavy; limited BAAS
•	Onafriq (MFS Africa): P2P remittance king, but not BAAS for businesses
•	Stripe Treasury / Unit / Treasury Prime: Global BAAS leaders, but zero African local rail depth
