Section 18.2 · Regulatory Disclosures

Directly licensed. Regulated worldwide.

Paymo is a financial technology company, not a bank. We operate through our own direct licenses and partner bank sponsorship.

🇳🇬
Nigeria

CBN Regulated

Licensed as a Payment Solution Service Provider (PSSP) and Switching Company. License No: PSSP/SW-2024-8839.

🇰🇪
Kenya

CBK Regulated

Regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya as a Payment Service Provider (PSP) and Money Remittance provider.

🇬🇭
Ghana

BoG Regulated

Holding an Electronic Money Issuer (EMI) license granted by the Bank of Ghana under the Payment Systems Act.

In South Africa, UAE, UK, and EU, banking services are provided by sponsored partner banks including Standard Bank, Emirates NBD, and Barclays.
