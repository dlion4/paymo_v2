Section 13.3 · Featured Deep-Dive

How KobaPay built a diaspora neobank in 11 weeks.

KB

KobaPay

Diaspora-focused neobank serving the UK ↔ Nigeria corridor with multi-currency accounts and instant remittance.

Fintech 🇬🇧 🇳🇬 Banking · FX · Cards
Challenge

Five separate vendors for KYC, FX, payouts, accounts, and cards. 5-day settlement, hidden FX spreads, and no unified ledger killed unit economics.

Solution

Single Paymo integration: virtual GBP/NGN accounts, intelligent FX locking, white-label cards, and embedded KYC/AML — one API, one contract.

Implementation

Sandbox to production in 11 weeks. Used Node SDK, pre-built KYC flows, and white-label mobile kit. First live transaction in 4 hours.

Results

60% lower remittance costs, 120K users in 9 months, 99.6% payout success, and a 4.8★ app store rating across both markets.
