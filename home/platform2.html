FAQ · Platform

Everything teams ask before they sign the contract.

Yes. Paymo is fully composable. You can start with just Payments, add Banking later, then flip on FX when your corridors expand. No forced bundling, no bloat in your integration surface.

Paymo stores transaction data in-region by default (eu-west-1, af-south-1, me-south-1). Enterprise plans support pinning data to specific jurisdictions to satisfy NDPR, POPIA, GDPR, and other data-localization requirements. Cross-border aggregation is available only under explicit customer configuration.

Most teams run Paymo in parallel for 2–4 weeks, routing a fraction of traffic before full cutover. We provide migration tooling for customer wallets, standing orders, and subscription state. Solutions engineers are on-call throughout.

Paymo uses volume-based, transparent pricing. Starter plans are pay-as-you-go. Growth and Scale plans unlock volume discounts. Enterprise plans support committed-use discounts with 12–36 month commitments and custom infrastructure pricing.

Paymo holds PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and ISO 22301 certifications. Customer funds are held in segregated FBO accounts with tier-1 partner banks. Secrets live in HSMs. All infrastructure runs on zero-trust networking. Penetration tests run quarterly and results are available to enterprise customers under NDA.

Enterprise plans include a dedicated solutions engineer, on-call dev-ops (24/7), quarterly architecture reviews, custom SLAs with financial credits, and co-branded incident communication. Mission-critical plans add dedicated infrastructure in private cloud or on-prem footprints.
