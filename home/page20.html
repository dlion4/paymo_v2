How We Use Your Data

Primary Purposes
  • Verifying identity via pan-African KYC registries.
  • Orchestrating settlements across 30+ rails.
  • Generating immutable audit trails for regulators.
  • Dispatching real-time security and fraud alerts.
Secondary Purposes
  • Refining AI-driven smart routing algorithms.
  • Generating anonymized corridor liquidity reports.
  • Predictive churn and usage pattern analytics.
  • Personalizing developer documentation.
Automated Decision Making

We use algorithmic risk scoring for instant KYC approvals. You have the right to request manual human review for any significant automated decision.

Data Sharing & International Transfers

To provide a borderless experience, your data may move between our secure nodes. We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Adequacy Decisions for all cross-border transfers.

Partner Banks

For escrow and settlement compliance.

Mobile Networks

For M-Pesa, MoMo, and SMS alerts.

Regulators

Central banks and tax authorities.

International Safeguards

Data center locations: AWS Cape Town (Primary), Azure Johannesburg (DR), AWS Ireland (Global Ops). All encrypted with AES-256 at rest.

Data Security Measures

Technical Measures
  • TLS 1.3 Encryption in Transit
  • Envelope Encryption (at Application Level)
  • Mandatory Multi-Factor Auth (MFA)
  • Quarterly NCC Group Pen Testing
Organizational Measures
  • Least-Privilege RBAC Models
  • Strict Employee Background Checks
  • 72-Hour Breach Notification SLA
  • Annual Security Awareness Training
