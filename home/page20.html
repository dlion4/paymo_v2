Section 22.2 · What Are Cookies

Small files. Big transparency.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website or use our apps. They help us remember your preferences, keep you logged in securely, understand how our services are used, and (with your consent) personalize content.

First-party cookies
Set by Paymo directly to run core platform functions.
Third-party cookies
Set by trusted partners for analytics and marketing (consent required).
Session vs. persistent
Session cookies expire when you close your browser; persistent ones remain for a set period.
Consent lifecycle
From banner to preference center to enforcement
