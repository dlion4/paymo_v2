Section 13.3 · Featured Story

How MintBank Africa launched across 5 markets in 87 days.

A complete case study of how a Lagos-based digital bank used Paymo BAAS to go from zero to live multi-market operations — replacing a fragmented stack of 7 providers.

The Challenge

Planning pan-African expansion but stuck between 7 payment providers, 3 KYC vendors, separate reconciliation per market, and hidden FX costs eroding margins by 3.2% per corridor.

The Solution

Replaced entire stack with Paymo: virtual accounts (USD, NGN, KES, GHS, ZAR), embedded KYC/KYB, smart rail routing, auto-reconciliation, and FX optimization engine — one API contract.

Implementation

Sandbox integration in week 1. Pilot in Nigeria (week 3). Kenya & Ghana parallel rollout (weeks 4–6). South Africa & Uganda final phase (weeks 8–11). Full production cutover at day 87 with automated migration scripts.

The Results

5 markets live in 87 days vs projected 18 months. FX cost reduced 74%. Reconciliation time from 40 hours/week to 10 minutes via automation. API latency avg 98ms. Zero security incidents post-launch.
