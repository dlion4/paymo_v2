Section 26.3 · What We Handle

Common complaint categories.

Pick the category that fits your situation — it helps us route your case to the right specialist faster.

Failed or Delayed Transaction

Payment didn't go through, settled late, or funds debited without delivery.

Duplicate / Erroneous Charge

You were charged twice or for the wrong amount. We investigate and refund where valid.

Fee Dispute

Disagree with a fee or FX rate applied. Must be raised within 15 days of the charge.

Unauthorized Activity

Suspected fraud or transactions you didn't make. Report within 24 hours.

Account Access / Freeze

Locked out, account suspended, or funds on hold. We explain and resolve.

Service Quality

Concerns about support, communication, downtime, or overall experience.

Section 26.4 · Resolution Process

Four tiers. Escalating only if needed.

Most cases resolve at Tier 1. If you're not satisfied, escalate to the next tier — all the way to independent regulators.

1
Frontline Support
<24h ack · 3 days

Reach our support team via chat, email, or the form below. Most issues are resolved here. You'll get a reference ID and a clear timeline.

2
Complaints Resolution Team
≤15 days

Unresolved or complex cases escalate to a dedicated specialist independent of the original handler. Formal written outcome provided.

3
Mediation
30 days

If you remain unsatisfied, both parties enter mandatory good-faith mediation before any formal proceedings — typically resolved within 30 days.

4
Arbitration / Regulatory
Final

Final-tier disputes go to binding arbitration (LCIA-MIAC Rules) or to your national financial regulator / ombudsman. See the regulators section below.

Section 26.7 · Independent Escalation

Not satisfied? Go independent.

If we can't resolve your complaint, you may escalate to the financial regulator or ombudsman in your jurisdiction at no cost.
