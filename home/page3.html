Section 3.8 · Retry & Failure Management

Failed once is not failed forever. Cascade. Recover. Settle.

Cascade fallback flow
  1. 01
    Primary provider attempt
    Highest-scoring rail per smart routing. Webhook-driven status.
  2. 02
    Soft failure detection
    Timeouts, transient errors, partial responses are classified automatically.
  3. 03
    Cascade to secondary rail
    Same currency, same recipient, idempotent. No double-charge possible.
  4. 04
    Tertiary & final fallback
    Bank EFT or alternate operator if mobile money rails are degraded.
  5. 05
    Settled & reconciled
    Final outcome, audit trail, and webhook fired in one envelope.
