Section 21.3 · Customer Due Diligence

Risk-based CDD. Three tiers of verification.

Our CDD framework applies simplified, standard, or enhanced due diligence based on customer risk profile, transaction patterns, and jurisdiction.

Tier 1
Simplified Due Diligence
Low-risk customers with limited transaction thresholds and basic identity verification.
  • Basic ID verification
  • Transaction limit: $1,000/month
  • No PEP/sanctions match
  • Low-risk jurisdiction
  • Automated approval
Tier 2
Standard Due Diligence
Standard KYC for most users — ID verification, address proof, PEP/sanctions screening.
  • Government ID + selfie
  • Address verification
  • PEP/sanctions screening
  • Transaction limit: $50,000/month
  • Automated + manual review
Tier 3
Enhanced Due Diligence
High-risk customers — additional documentation, source of funds, ongoing monitoring, senior management approval.
  • Source of funds/wealth
  • Enhanced background checks
  • Ongoing transaction monitoring
  • Senior management approval
  • Quarterly risk review

Section 21.8 · Prohibited Activities

Strictly forbidden. Zero exceptions.

Money Laundering

Concealing origins of illegally obtained money through complex transactions.

Terrorist Financing

Providing funds for terrorist acts or organizations, directly or indirectly.

Sanctions Evasion

Attempting to bypass OFAC, UN, EU, or local sanctions through any means.

Identity Fraud

Using false, stolen, or synthetic identities to open accounts or transact.

Unlicensed Activities

Operating without required licenses (lending, deposit-taking, gambling).

Illegal Goods/Services

Sale of drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods, or other prohibited items.

Structuring

Breaking transactions into smaller amounts to avoid reporting thresholds.

Unauthorized Automation

Using bots, scrapers, or automated systems without API authorization.

Reselling Services

Reselling Paymo services without explicit partnership agreement.

Enforcement Actions

Violations result in immediate account suspension, fund freezing (up to 180 days per regulatory requirements), law enforcement referral, and permanent platform ban. Legal action may be pursued where applicable.
