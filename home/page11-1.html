SECTION 11.9 · FAQ

Coverage questions, answered.

Which African markets are currently live on Paymo BAAS?+

Paymo BAAS is live in Nigeria, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and South Africa — with Benin, Togo, Burkina Faso, Namibia, Botswana, and DR Congo launching on demand for enterprise rollouts. Global hubs in London, New York, Frankfurt, Dubai, and Singapore are production-ready for treasury-grade payouts.

What local mobile-money schemes are supported?+

Paymo normalizes M-Pesa (KE · TZ · others), MTN MoMo (GH · CI · SN), Airtel Money (UG · KE), Orange Money (CI · SN), Wave (SN · CI), Tigo Pesa (TZ), Vodafone Cash, Paga (NG), Opay (NG), and bank account-based intakes (NIBSS NIP, GhIPSS, EFT) into a single collections and payouts API.

How does Paymo differ from a single payment gateway?+

Most gateways expose one rail per market. Paymo is an orchestration + BAAS layer: it combines local rails, global corridors, virtual accounts, a double-entry ledger, card programs, dynamic FX, and embedded compliance into one integration surface. You build against Paymo — not against five regional vendors.

What does a corridor rollout look like?+

A corridor onboarding usually takes 2–4 weeks after KYB and licensing alignment: sandbox credentials, API keys, sample collections/payouts, live KYC/KYB, and a go-live review window. Enterprise rollouts spanning multiple corridors typically complete in 90 days.

Can I hold balances in multiple currencies?+

Yes. Paymo BAAS supports virtual accounts and held balances in NGN, KES, GHS, XOF, TZS, UGX, RWF, ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP, and AED. Paymo also provides treasury-aware auto-conversion between balances when a payout corridor demands a specific settlement currency.

How is route reliability guaranteed?+

Every Paymo route has a primary rail plus a pre-integrated fallback rail. The intelligence layer continuously scores rail quality (latency, failure rate, FX spread, settlement window) and re-routes in real time when degradation is detected — keeping payout success above 99.9% across live corridors.
