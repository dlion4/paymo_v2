01
Processing Instructions

Paymo shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law.

  • Processing limited to the scope set out in Annex 1.
  • Paymo immediately informs the Controller if an instruction infringes data protection law.
  • No processing for Paymo's own commercial purposes.
02
Confidentiality

Paymo ensures that all personnel authorized to process personal data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

  • Contractual confidentiality commitments for all staff and contractors.
  • Access strictly on a need-to-know, least-privilege basis.
  • Mandatory data protection training, refreshed annually.
03
Security Measures

Paymo implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk (detailed in Annex 2 and Section 23.6 below).

  • AES-256 encryption at rest; TLS 1.3 in transit.
  • Pseudonymization and access controls for sensitive fields.
  • Resilience, backup, and tested disaster recovery (RPO <5m, RTO <15m).
04
Sub-Processing

The Controller grants general authorization for Paymo to engage sub-processors, subject to the conditions below.

  • Current sub-processor list maintained and published (Section 23.4).
  • 30 days' advance notice of new or replacement sub-processors.
  • Right to object on reasonable data-protection grounds.
  • Flow-down of equivalent data protection obligations by written contract.
05
Assistance with Data Subject Rights

Paymo provides reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights.

  • Tooling to access, rectify, export, restrict, and erase personal data.
  • Forwarding of any direct data-subject requests to the Controller.
  • Machine-readable data portability exports on request.
06
Personal Data Breach Notification

Paymo notifies the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's data.

  • Notification includes nature, categories, approximate numbers, and likely consequences.
  • Description of measures taken or proposed to address the breach.
  • Dedicated incident contact and 24/7 Security Operations Center.
07
DPIAs & Prior Consultation

Paymo assists the Controller in carrying out Data Protection Impact Assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities where required.

  • Provision of relevant processing documentation.
  • Technical and security information to support risk assessment.
08
Return & Deletion of Data

Upon termination, Paymo deletes or returns all personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.

  • Choice of secure deletion or export in a structured format.
  • Financial records retained for the regulatory period (typically 5–7 years).
  • Backup data purged within 90 days of deletion request.
09
Audits & Inspections

Paymo makes available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allows for audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an appointed auditor.

  • Annual SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 reports available under NDA.
  • On-site audits permitted with reasonable notice (max once/year, or post-incident).
  • Audit costs borne by the Controller unless material non-compliance is found.
10
Liability & Governing Law

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set out in the main agreement. This DPA is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with SCCs applying to relevant international transfers.

  • Liability cap aligns with the underlying Terms of Use.
  • SCCs prevail in the event of conflict for restricted transfers.
  • Severability: invalid provisions do not affect the remainder.

Section 23.5 · International Transfers

Cross-border data, lawfully safeguarded.

Standard Contractual Clauses

EU Commission-approved SCCs (2021 modules) for transfers from EEA/UK to third countries.

Adequacy Decisions

Reliance on adequacy where a destination country offers equivalent protection.

Binding Corporate Rules

Intra-group transfers governed by approved BCRs where applicable.

Data Localization

Regional storage options (NG, KE, ZA, EU, US) for regulatory localization needs.

Transfer impact assessed
Safeguarded cross-border data flows
Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs)

For each restricted transfer, we conduct a TIA evaluating the destination's legal framework and apply supplementary measures (encryption, pseudonymization) as needed.

Section 23.6 · Technical & Organizational Measures

Annex 2 — security, codified.

Encryption

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, application-layer encryption for PII.

Access Control

RBAC, least privilege, MFA enforced, session-recorded admin access.

Pseudonymization

Tokenization of card data, masked PII in logs and non-prod environments.

Resilience

Multi-region active-active, RPO <5m, RTO <15m, tested DR drills.

Monitoring

24/7 SOC, SIEM, EDR, intrusion detection, anomaly alerting.

Testing

Quarterly pen tests, bug bounty, SAST/DAST on every deploy.

Certifications

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, PCI DSS Level 1, ISO 22301.

Staff Training

Annual data protection training, background checks, NDAs.
