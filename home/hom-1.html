Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know — before you ship.

Sandbox access is instant — you can make your first test transaction in about 4.2 minutes. Most teams go live in their primary market within 7 days. Full multi-market rollout with treasury optimization typically completes within 30–90 days.

No setup fees. No monthly minimums for startups on the Starter plan. Growth ($99/mo) and Scale ($499/mo) plans unlock advanced markets, white-label components, and priority support. Enterprise pricing is fully custom and volume-based.

All customer funds are held in segregated For-Benefit-Of (FBO) accounts with tier-1 partner banks. These funds are bankruptcy-remote and never part of Paymo's operating balance sheet. Paymo maintains cyber-liability and E&O insurance in excess of $5M.

Paymo is a licensed Payment Solution Service Provider (PSSP) under the Central Bank of Nigeria, a graduate of the Central Bank of Kenya regulatory sandbox, and holds PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and ISO 22301 certifications. In other markets, we operate through licensed partner banks and mobile-money operators.

Yes. Paymo offers full white-labeling on Growth plans and above: branded virtual and physical cards, customizable onboarding flows, co-branded dashboards, embeddable checkout widgets, and white-label mobile SDKs. Your customers never need to know we exist unless you want them to.

Paymo aggregates transaction volume to access wholesale FX liquidity. Typical spreads sit between 0.15% and 0.35% — roughly 8–12x better than the 2–4% spreads most African businesses pay at retail banks. Every rate is transparent and lockable for 30 seconds before execution.
