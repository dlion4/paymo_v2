01 — Unit Story

A high-visibility ground-floor shop for daily walk-in customers.

Unit A2 is one of the most practical commercial spaces at Kamakis Heights because it combines a road-facing entrance with a separate back room. The front room works well as a display or service area, while the rear room can operate as stock storage, a treatment room, private office, or staff area.

The unit faces the Kamakis–Juja access road, with foot traffic from residents, boda riders, nearby stalls, and commuters using the Eastern Bypass stage. It has a roll-up shutter door, tiled flooring, internal lighting points, KPLC prepaid meter, two windows, and access to the shared commercial washroom corridor.

It is currently vacant and can be viewed daily by appointment. Fresh repainting can be discussed with the caretaker before move-in, depending on the tenant's business type and signage needs.

07 — Lease Terms

Shop tenancy rules for Unit A2.

Tenant must provide National ID/passport, KRA PIN, business name or business permit where available, next-of-kin details, and two passport photos. Move-in requires KES 57,000 minimum before key handover.

Allowed: salon, barber, boutique, cosmetics, phone accessories, cyber, parcel office, M-Pesa, agency office, small pharmacy subject to county approvals. Restricted: bar/nightclub, loud church, heavy welding, butchery without drainage approval, gambling shop, or any business that disturbs residential tenants.

Normal operating hours are 5 AM to 11 PM. Operations beyond 11 PM need prior written approval from the owner and caretaker. After-hours access is logged by the night watchman.

External signage is allowed on the assigned fascia after approval. Drilling, plumbing alterations, wall demolition, or electrical rewiring must be approved by the caretaker and done by a licensed technician. Tenant bears cost of business-specific branding.

Tenant gives one full calendar month notice to vacate. Deposit is refunded within 30 days after final inspection, less unpaid rent, unpaid bills, repainting, broken fixtures, or unapproved modifications.
