Unit Overview

A light-filled corner residence on the 2nd floor.

Unit C3 is the largest two-bedroom apartment on the 2nd floor, occupying the north-east corner of the building. Five large windows flood the apartment with natural light throughout the day. The wrap-around balcony overlooks the Eastern Bypass and the Kamakis hills beyond.

The unit features a master bedroom with en-suite shower and toilet, a second bedroom, a separate guest toilet, a spacious living/dining area, and a modern kitchen with a pantry nook. All floors are porcelain tile, and ceilings are gypsum with LED downlights.

The apartment is freshly painted in warm off-white. It is being offered unfurnished, though the previous tenant left behind curtain rods and a kitchen shelf that can be kept at no extra cost.

Natural Light & Views

The apartment is positioned on the north-east corner, receiving morning sun through the kitchen and living room, and afternoon light in both bedrooms. The 5 windows are:

  • 1 × Large sliding window in living room (road-facing)
  • 1 × Window in dining area (corner, 270° view)
  • 2 × Windows in master bedroom (road-facing)

All windows are double-glazed aluminium casements with mosquito netting. Curtain rods are pre-installed.
