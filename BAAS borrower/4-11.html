Payments FAQ

Common questions about loan repayments
How do I make an early settlement?
You can pay off your loan early via the 'My Loans' dashboard. Select 'Pay Off Total' to see the exact amount including any interest adjustments.
Which payment methods are accepted?
We accept M-Pesa, Bank Transfer (PesaLink), and Direct Debit from linked accounts.
